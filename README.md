# QuickCharts - HACKGT12 Project Submission

QuickCharts helps healthcare professionals send pre-appointment questionnaires to patients and gather relevant information ahead of time. It uses a large language model to adapt its questions to each patient's concerns and symptoms, then generates a concise summary for both the patient and the HCP — saving time on both sides and helping HCPs get up to speed before the visit even starts.

## Inspiration

We wanted to build a full-stack web app that played to our strengths as a team. The Impricius sponsor challenge, paired with the curator's cause, was the perfect opportunity to showcase our skills while building something genuinely meaningful.

## What it does

QuickCharts gives healthcare professionals the tools to send questionnaires to patients ahead of their appointments to gather relevant data about their situation. It leverages a large language model to provide adaptive responses to each user's concerns and symptoms, then generates a short summary for both the patient and the HCP. Over the long run, this saves patients and HCPs a tremendous amount of time — HCPs can quickly grasp the situation and provide the best possible treatment.

## How we built it

QuickCharts is built with a Spring Boot / Java backend paired with a Vue.js and Tailwind CSS frontend. Together, these let us quickly put together a full-stack app capable of hosting a large language model that delivers high-quality feedback to many users at once.

## Challenges we ran into

Version control was new to some members of the team, which led to a few merge conflicts (and some embarrassing setbacks) along the way. We pushed through and came out more comfortable with collaborative git workflows.

## Accomplishments that we're proud of

We're proud of the app we built — coming together as a team with no plan, having found each other through the matchmaking site. Despite varying levels of experience and different areas of expertise, we worked well together and shipped something we're proud of.

## What we learned

We learned a lot about working with a team in real time, mapping out larger projects, and breaking them down into manageable individual tasks. Several of us also got to try new tools and frameworks for the first time.

## What's next for QuickCharts

We don't plan to stop at the end of HackGT12. We see the potential in QuickCharts and hope to add more features that improve coordination and communication between patients and HCPs.
